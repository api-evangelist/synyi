# Synyi (森亿智能 / Shanghai Synyi Medical Technology)

> Synyi is a Shanghai-based medical artificial-intelligence company founded in April 2016 that
> builds AI and big-data systems for hospitals. Its platform standardizes and mines the
> unstructured clinical records held in Chinese hospital information systems using medical NLP,
> knowledge graphs and multi-agent LLM applications, and sells that capability as deployed
> hospital software across clinical decision support, medical-record quality control, hospital
> operations management and clinical research. IDC's "China Healthcare Big Data Solution Market
> Share, 2023" ranked Synyi first in its market.

IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS AND INTEGRATORS: Synyi publishes NO public API, NO developer portal, NO
API documentation and NO machine-readable contract. As of 2026-08-29 there is no OpenAPI,
GraphQL, AsyncAPI, gRPC or WSDL contract on any Synyi host, no MCP server, and no A2A agent
card. Synyi's products are enterprise systems deployed inside customer hospitals and reached
through a sales conversation, not through a self-serve API. Do not attempt to construct
requests against a Synyi API — none is published. See "Contract discovery" below for the exact
probes and their HTTP statuses.

## Products and solutions
- AI 诊所 (AI Clinic) — https://www.synyi.com/solution?id=43
  End-to-end AI-driven care model: voice-based intake and history taking, AI-directed physical
  examination via connected devices (temperature, laryngoscope, heart/lung sounds), assisted
  diagnosis, risk alerting and guideline-conformant treatment recommendation, with a
  human-in-the-loop safety layer of clinical rules, pathways and multi-agent cross-validation.
- 临床辅助决策与质控智能体 AI (Clinical decision support and quality-control agent AI)
  Monitors the full treatment and medical-record pipeline against hospital data, raising
  automated defect alerts and managing them to closure.
- 医院运营管理智能体 AI (Hospital operations management agent AI) — https://www.synyi.com/solution?id=36
  Conversational metric monitoring, automated root-cause analysis, 24x7 alerting with pushed
  intervention plans, and one-click operations reporting.
- 临床科研智能体 AI (Clinical research agent AI) — https://www.synyi.com/solution?id=34
  Natural-language cohort search, automated research-grade data cleaning, built-in analysis
  prompts, patient recruitment and enrollment, and automated form filling.
- 新一代医生工作站 (Next-generation clinician workstation), 临床质量管理 (clinical quality
  management), 指挥调度中心 (command and dispatch center), 绩效与效能管理 (performance
  management), 精益运营决策分析 (lean operations analytics), 区域数据及资源协同 (regional data
  and resource coordination).

## Contract discovery — what was probed and what it returned (2026-08-29)
- https://www.synyi.com/openapi.json, /swagger.json, /api-docs, /docs, /api, /developer,
  /llms.txt, /sitemap.xml — every one returns HTTP 302 to the site root. The host serves a
  blanket catch-all redirect; a control probe of /nonexistent-abc123 returns the identical 302.
- https://api.synyi.com/ and /openapi.json, /swagger.json, /v1/openapi.json, /api-docs,
  /v2/api-docs, /docs, /mcp — HTTP 503 (nginx vhost with no upstream).
- https://open.synyi.com/ — HTTP 200 serving the stock "Welcome to nginx!" default page;
  /openapi.json, /swagger.json and /docs return 404. Same for developer., docs., openapi.
  (all subdomains resolve through a wildcard record to the same two AWS China addresses).
- https://www.synyi.com/.well-known/{security.txt, openid-configuration,
  oauth-authorization-server, oauth-protected-resource, api-catalog, ai-plugin.json,
  agent-card.json, agent.json} — all HTTP 302 to the site root. No document is served.
- No GraphQL endpoint, no MCP server, no A2A agent card was found on any host.
- https://www.synyi.com/en — HTTP 302 to the site root. Search engines still index English
  pages such as /en/product-categories/13.html, but the English site has been withdrawn and
  every one of those URLs now redirects to the Chinese homepage.
